# BusBeacon Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: January 16, 2026**

## Overview

BusBeacon ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the BusBeacon mobile application.

## Our Privacy Philosophy

**Privacy First**: BusBeacon is designed with privacy as a core principle. We believe you should have full control over your data.

**No Data Collection**: We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data.

## Information We Do NOT Collect

BusBeacon does not collect:
- Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
- Location data sent to servers
- Usage analytics or statistics
- Device identifiers
- Advertising IDs
- Crash reports containing personal data
- Any other personally identifiable information

## Information Used Locally

BusBeacon uses the following information **only on your device**:

### Location Data
- **Purpose**: Display nearby bus stops on your screen
- **Storage**: Used in real-time, not stored permanently
- **Sharing**: Never sent to any server or third party
- **Control**: You can revoke location permission at any time in device settings

### App Preferences
- **What**: Language selection, beacon preferences, accessibility settings
- **Storage**: Stored locally on your device only
- **Sharing**: Never sent anywhere
- **Control**: Delete by uninstalling the app or clearing app data

## Permissions Required

### Location Permission (Optional)
- **Why**: To show nearby bus stops
- **How Used**: GPS coordinates are used to calculate distances to bus stops
- **Data Sent**: None - all processing happens on your device
- **Can Opt Out**: Yes - the app works without location, you just won't see nearby stops

### Notification Permission (Optional)
- **Why**: To notify you when beacon is active
- **How Used**: Local notifications only
- **Data Sent**: None
- **Can Opt Out**: Yes - disable in Settings

### Camera Permission (Optional)
- **Why**: To use flash as additional beacon signal
- **How Used**: Only to control camera flash, no photos/videos taken
- **Data Sent**: None
- **Can Opt Out**: Yes - beacon works without flash

## Third-Party Services

**None**: BusBeacon does not use any third-party services, analytics, advertising networks, or cloud services.

## Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device using:
- iOS: UserDefaults (for settings)
- Android: SharedPreferences (for settings)

This data:
- Remains on your device only
- Is not backed up to cloud services (by default)
- Is deleted when you uninstall the app
- Is not accessible to other apps

## Data Sharing

We do not share any data with:
- Third parties
- Advertisers
- Analytics companies
- Other apps
- Anyone

**Exception**: If required by law, we may be compelled to provide information. However, since we don't collect any data, we have nothing to provide.

## Children's Privacy

BusBeacon is safe for all ages. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

The app complies with:
- Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)
- General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
- California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)

## Your Rights

Since we don't collect data, you automatically have full control:

### Right to Access
There is no data about you to access.

### Right to Deletion
Uninstall the app to delete all local settings.

### Right to Opt-Out
You control all permissions in device settings.

### Right to Data Portability
Settings are stored locally and not transferable.

## Security

### Device Security
- Permissions only requested when needed
- Settings encrypted by OS
- No custom data collection code

## Privacy by Design

BusBeacon is built with "Privacy by Design" principles:

1. **Proactive not Reactive**: Privacy is built in from the start
2. **Privacy as Default**: Maximum privacy is the default setting
3. **Privacy Embedded**: Privacy is integral to app design
4. **Full Functionality**: Privacy doesn't limit core features
5. **End-to-End Security**: Local processing, no data transmission
6. **Visibility and Transparency**: Open source and clear policies
7. **User-Centric**: You control all permissions and settings
